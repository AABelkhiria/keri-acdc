## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-10

First release, extracted from the signify-rs project with its history.

### Added

- The ACDC credential container and its attribute block, with the two self-addressing identifiers
  that give a credential its identity.
- The `iss` and `rev` transaction events, the seal that binds them to the issuer's key event log,
  the unsigned interaction event anchoring that seal, and the CESR attachments chaining each
  artifact to the next.
- The five IPEX exchange messages (`apply`, `offer`, `agree`, `grant`, `admit`), the disclosure a
  grant carries, and the signed-message and signed-anchor types that carry a caller's signatures.
- Cross-language fixtures generated by [signify-ts], pinned by commit through npm.

[signify-ts]: https://github.com/WebOfTrust/signify-ts
